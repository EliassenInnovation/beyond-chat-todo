# Build your coding-agent handoff

Choose and adapt the [acceptance checks](../principles/implementation.md) for one agreed
job. Each check explains what to inspect. The [Apply chapter](apply.md) helps you define
the work first. These choices describe your scope; they do not establish that the app passes.

This builder runs in your browser. Your selections and notes are kept in memory and are
not sent to a server or saved after a reload. Download a draft to retain unresolved decisions, then load `agent-checks.md` here to resume
on this computer or another. Loading replaces the current selections.
Keep credentials and personal data out of the handoff.

and **agent-checks.md**, containing selected checks and every decision. Optional chapters
can travel with the checks. Review the preview before downloading. Undecided checks stay
visible in draft handoffs; they are not silently excluded or approved.
